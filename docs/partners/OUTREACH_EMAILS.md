# Outreach emails

Three short emails for Murat to personalise and send himself. Each has an English and a Turkish
version, under 150 words. Replace every `[placeholder]`. Send each one from your own address,
one person at a time.

Before sending:

- Only say you know someone if you do. Name where you met in one line, or leave that line out.
- Keep the ask small: a 20-minute call, or a look at the demo and the sample report.
- Do not attach files. Link to the public pages:
  - Demo: https://attackledger.com/demo/
  - Sample report: https://attackledger.com/sample-report.html
  - Verifier: https://attackledger.com/verify
- If they want more, send the one-pager ([ONE_PAGER.md](ONE_PAGER.md) or `one-pager.html`
  printed to PDF).
